Shown as: Default follows the website code (first drop live: Vol. 01, Studio Mini, Wedge, Tape Case, B-Side, Rotary. Track Plate, Track Tag and Side A second drop, the rest coming soon). Live makes it orderable in the current phase. Hidden removes it from the site. Max per order limits how many one customer can add (empty = 5). Order cap is the total this drop can take: every order that is not rejected, cancelled or refunded holds its slots, and the product shows Sold out by itself at the cap. A rejected or cancelled order, or one not paid in time, gives its slots back. It needs the Orders email address set above; without it the cap is not applied. Use Sold out only to close a product by hand.
