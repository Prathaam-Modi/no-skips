How to order

  1. Pick your pieces

    Open a product, add your song, name or date, and choose an ivory or red accent.

  2. Send it on WhatsApp

    One tap opens WhatsApp with your order already written out. For song pieces, the song title, artist and, for a Spotify Code, the Spotify link go in the message.

  3. Approve and pay

    Personalised pieces get a preview before anything is printed. Payment is by UPI.

  4. Printed, numbered, shipped

    Every piece is printed to order in Bengaluru. The code on the box opens the build steps. Shipping is quoted in the chat.

Shipping and packing

Every order ships in a No Skips box with tissue and a build card. Pieces in one order share a box. Shipping is charged on top and quoted in the chat with the dispatch date.

Replacements within 48 hours

Pieces are made to order, so we do not take returns. If something arrives broken, faulty or not as approved, message us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and an unboxing video and we replace it.

Checked before it ships

Every personalised piece is reviewed by hand against your order, and you see the preview before it is printed.
