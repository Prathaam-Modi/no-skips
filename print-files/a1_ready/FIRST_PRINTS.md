# No Skips - first prints plan

One A1, about a week of evenings. Cheapest and most informative prints first. Do not open pre-orders for a product until it passes here.

## Day 1: fit coupon (1.4 h, 39 g, black only)
File: 00_fit_coupon/00_fit_coupon.3mf
- Block with five round holes (6.1 to 6.5 mm, tick marks count up), four slots (6.2 to 6.5), three sockets for the Hold tongue (30.2, 30.3, 30.5), plus two 6.00 pins, a 6.00 plate, a 30 x 10 tongue and a dovetail with sockets at 0.15, 0.25 and 0.35 mm a side.
- Write down which hole gives a firm hand press for the pin, which slot holds the plate without wobble, which socket takes the tongue, and which dovetail slides without rattling.
- Pass: the fits the range uses (0.1 press, 0.2 to 0.25 slide) are the ones that feel right. If your printer wants more or less, tell me the numbers and I will shift the clearances in CAD before anything else is printed.

## Day 1 to 2: one Studio Mini (0.8 h)
- Mini 01 turntable pass: red accents clean, no black bleeding into red, number readable underneath.

## Day 2 to 3: the other five minis (5.3 h) and the Vol. 01 tray (1.7 h)
- Pass: each mini drops into its pocket without forcing and does not slide; mic stands straight after supports come off; headphone band presses into its base. Tray lies flat (no corner lift on a 196 mm part) and the lettering is sharp.
- Decide here how to print the headphone ear pads (38 colour changes in one job).

## Day 3: Rail (1.8 h) and Tape Case (1.3 h)
- Rail pass: all five pegs press in by hand and do not pull out with a firm tug; rail is flat against a wall; name inlay clean.
- Tape Case pass: lid presses on and stays on when held upside down; lid comes off with one hand; label inlay flush.

## Day 4: Hold (7.8 h over two plates)
- Pass: post presses into base and saddle with no wobble and no glue; stands a 350 g pair of headphones for 24 hours without leaning; post printed lying down has no visible warp.

## Day 4 add-on: Dock (2.6 h over two plates, only after Hold passes)
- Pass: drops over the post past the fader without catching and sits flat on the knob; red caps slide on and do not fall off when the Dock is turned over (if loose, the coupon dovetail result sets DOVE in build6.py); a DualSense or Xbox controller hangs in each cradle and clears the ear cups of your own headphones; with one controller in front and no headphones, the stand does not tip when the desk is bumped.

## Day 5: second drop
- Track Tag and Track Plate from a real order-kit file: the code must scan in the Spotify app from 20 to 30 cm, three times out of three, before and after rubbing with keys. Side A: the rail slides and links.

## After that
Stack, B-Side, Gain, Alias, Rotary, Eject, Drumroll (PLA parts, then the 8 mm TPU belt), Crate, Lift, Horn, Sleeve, Fold, Channel, Standby. PETG pieces (45s, Perch) last, with a hot-cup test.

## What to send back
For each print: a photo, the Bambu Studio time and grams from the finished job, and a pass or fail per line above. Those numbers replace the slicer figures in the cost sheet.
